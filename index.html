SSTV,
on a schedule.

SSTV sends a still picture over a narrowband voice channel, one line at a time. Estivi turns that into a channel you program: lay out a week of pictures, bumpers, and one-off blocks; the transmitter keys the radio and plays it, callsign and all. The browser receiver turns the air back into pictures and a calendar.

01 The channel

No cloud.
Just a carrier.

Estivi is three repositories and a radio link. The transmitter holds the week, keys the radio, and answers CW and DTMF. The receiver turns the audio back into pictures, a guide, and a calendar. The umbrella pins both at known commits.

03 Both ends

Built for
both ends.

One half behaves like a station. The other behaves like a radio. Every feature is either a better transmission or a better reception — and nothing needs a server in the middle.

TX · pictures 01

→
pictureM1
→
bumper postR12

byeR24

A picture with a cast.

Welcome and bye go out on the first and last turn. Pre- and post-bumpers travel with their picture, which can inherit its mode or override it.

TX · triggers 02
CW K DTMF 5
→
picture command API reply sequence

It answers the radio.

CW and DTMF triggers can run a picture, a command, an API reply, or a sequence of actions.

TX · live 03
next 00:25:25 atardecer.png

Live and next.

Watch the picture fill line by line while it goes out. The next turn is already waiting with its clock and file name.

RX · decoder 04
audio→audio worklet→AX.25 + SSTV→pixels
0 decoders on the server

Decode in the page.

Radio audio runs through a gain stage into an AudioWorklet. AX.25 and SSTV are JavaScript; the server only serves static files.

RX · signal 05
signalfadepick up
waits 30% of the picture

Hold a bad signal together.

A short fade does not throw the picture away. The decoder waits 30% of the mode’s picture time and picks up where the signal returns.

RX · offline 06
rx. estivi
installed offline ready pictures in IndexedDB

Installs like an app.

Chromium offers Install app; iPhone and iPad add it to the home screen. A service worker keeps the page, pictures, and guide available offline.

TX · package 07
LinuxWindowsmacOS
~50 MB RAM
7 themesEN / ESCSV log

Small enough to forget.

One executable, software-rendered in about 50 MB of RAM. Seven themes, English or Spanish, and a CSV log of every transmission.

04 On the air

The air is
the protocol.

There is no private link between the two ends. Everything the receiver knows arrives as sound: SSTV pictures with their VIS headers, and 1200-baud AX.25 UI frames addressed to ESTIVI. A listener who misses the guide still gets the pictures; the calendar fills in as the frames arrive.

Modes
22, from Martin to YoniQ

A frame carries the station name, frequency, range, days, days left, and what is next. Mode frames name each picture, its bumpers, welcome, and bye.

receiver · estivi-rx

browser

Hear the channel.

Open the public receiver, allow the microphone, and point it at your radio’s speaker or line audio. Decoding happens in the page; the server only serves static files.

estivi-rx.lu1aqy.radio

Microphone access needs HTTPS or localhost. It installs as an app and opens offline after the first visit.

run your own
git clone --recurse-submodules https://github.com/fluoroom/estivi.git
cd estivi/estivi-rx
docker compose up -d --build

opens on http://localhost:8086
